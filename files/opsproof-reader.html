Check a Record

A Run Record is the signed account of one piece of work done by people, AI agents and machines together. Drop one here to see whether it holds up and what happened. Drop a folder of them to see what ran: what finished, what failed, where a person stepped in, and, with the workspace's register, whether any are missing. Everything is checked in this page: no Record leaves your computer.

Check it came from who you expect

The same checker runs offline from the command line: runrec verify record.runrec --pin sha256:… for one Record, runrec report folder/ --register workspace.register.json for what ran and what is missing. The format is open and published with its rules, so anyone can write their own checker, and an independent one in Python already agrees with this one.
